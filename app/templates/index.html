State AI Readiness Index

Rankings look like facts. They are choices.

Reports rank states on how ready they are for AI in schools. Every one of them picks what to measure, then picks how much each thing counts. Someone chooses those numbers.

Change them and the order changes. Most reports never show you that. This site hands you the controls, and measures how much the ranking moves when you use them.

All the data is public, and every number links to where it came from.

An index is a choice, not a measurement.

Rankings like this one help decide where attention and money go. But every ranking depends on two choices: which things to measure, and how much each one counts. Someone picks those. Change them and the ranking changes. Most reports don't show you that. This one lets you do it yourself.
