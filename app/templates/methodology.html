How the index works, and where it fails

Two decisions turn these indicators into a ranking. Both are arguable, and this page names them rather than putting them in an appendix.

1. Normalisation

The inputs are not comparable. A grade-8 maths scale score near 283 and a policy status of "issued" are different kinds of number, so putting them in one average means deciding what counts as 0 and what counts as 100.

Min-max makes the best state 100 and the worst 0. It reads clearly and it is what most published indices use — but if every state falls between 78 and 82, it stretches four real points across the whole scale and makes near-ties look decisive. Z-scores keep the true spread, so an average state lands in the middle instead of at an extreme, at the cost of a scale with no natural ends. Neither is correct. You can switch between them and watch what changes.

What we do with missing data

Nothing. A state absent from a dataset stays absent. It is scored on the indicators it has, its weights renormalised over those, and its coverage is shown so a score resting on partial evidence is visibly doing so. Imputing a value would invent a measurement, and an index that quietly does that is the thing this project argues against.

What this does not show

It does not show that technology or AI policy causes educational outcomes. These indicators are separately measured and heavily confounded — wealthier states tend to do better on all of them at once, and nothing here separates that out. A state near the top has good test results and has published guidance. That is all it means.

Where it is weak

  • NAEP runs every two years. 2024 is the current administration, so this is a snapshot, not a trend, and it cannot show whether anything is improving.
  • "No guidance found" is not "no guidance". The policy indicator records absence from a tracker, which is weaker evidence than it looks.
  • The indicator set is short. Broadband and device access belong here and are not yet in — see Sources for what is loaded and what is missing.
  • Rank instability is measured against the alternatives we chose. A wider set of defensible recipes would likely produce a wider swing, not a narrower one.
